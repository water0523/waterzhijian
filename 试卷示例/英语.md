## 第二部分　阅读（题型示例）

阅读下列短文，从每题所给的 A、B、C、D 四个选项中选出最佳选项。

:::exam-passage A · Saturday Book Care
Our community library is looking for volunteers aged sixteen and over. Every Saturday from ten to twelve, a small team checks returned books and repairs damaged covers. You do not need previous experience. A librarian will provide a short training session before your first task.

The project started when student readers noticed that several favourite books could no longer be borrowed. Instead of buying new copies immediately, they asked whether the old ones could be repaired. Their idea has now become a regular activity. Volunteers say that careful work has helped them understand how shared resources can last longer.

If you would like to join, email the library with your name and the dates when you are available.
:::

:::exam-question 2. What is the main purpose of the activity?（2.5 分）
:::exam-options 1
- A. To sell old books.
- B. To care for shared library books.
- C. To train professional writers.
- D. To build a new library.
:::
:::

:::exam-question 3. What will new volunteers receive before their first task?（2.5 分）
:::exam-options 2
- A. A free book.
- B. A paid position.
- C. A short training session.
- D. A writing award.
:::
:::

On my first day as a library volunteer, I found a small note inside a damaged book. It said, “This story helped me through a difficult week. Please pass it on.” I did not know who had written it, but I decided to repair the cover as carefully as I could. When I finished, an elderly reader came to the desk and asked whether the book was ready.
